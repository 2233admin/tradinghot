# Brand facts: 明日方舟 / Arknights

Declared by the owner as the design reference for the site's redesign (2026-11-14).
Every line carries its source; anything unsourced is not stated as fact.

## 明日方舟 / Arknights

- Arknights is Hypergryph's released mobile game, published by Yostar, with an official global
  site at <https://arknights.global/> (source: https://arknights.global/).
- The official site's display type is **Oswald** (Bold / DemiBold) for headings, **Novecento
  Medium** for UI numerals, and **Noto Sans CJK SC** for Chinese text — measured from the page's
  computed styles on 2026-11-14 (source: .design-pipeline/reference/arknights/extraction.json).
- The site's measured primary colour is **#a3c4d5**, a desaturated cold blue (HSL saturation 0.37,
  hue ≈ 200°), against a neutral base with dark panel steps at **#3b3b3b** and **#1a1a1a**
  (source: .design-pipeline/reference/arknights/palette-evidence.json).
- The official site is the source used for type, colour and density; the in-game interface screen
  was **not** measured and no fact about it is claimed here.
- The amber/orange accent associated with Arknights artwork was **not** found in the page's
  computed styles; it is not adopted as a token without a measured source.

## What the owner asked for (not a brand fact, their instruction)

- The site's redesign should follow Arknights' design language and palette, not the current cream
  theme and not the current near-black/cyan dark theme (owner, 2026-11-14).
- The site is a second-hand use of a game studio's visual language for a finance news site; the
  reference supplies structure, type and colour discipline, not characters or artwork.
